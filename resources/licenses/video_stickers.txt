scripts/build_sticker_codecs.sh builds the Matroska demuxer, VP9 parser,
libvpx VP9 decoder and their utility code for the stickers, and the libvpx VP8
encoder and decoder for video calls (core/video/exteria_vp8.c). Networking, the
VP9 encoder, other codecs, assembly, libswscale and newer-runtime hardware
integration are disabled.
The complete corresponding library sources and build script must accompany a
distribution, or be made available as required by their licenses.
