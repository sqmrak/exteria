emoji.pack holds the pictures of every emoji, so the app shows one set on every system.
The pictures are Apple's iOS 26.4 emoji (Unicode 17), taken from the font in
https://github.com/Keinta15/Magisk-iOS-Emoji (system/fonts/NotoColorEmoji.ttf), reduced to 56 pixels and stored as WebP.
They are Apple's artwork and are not licensed for redistribution: do not publish an ipa or a repository that carries this pack.
The 19 emoji of Unicode 18.0 that the font lacks keep the Noto Emoji 3D pictures, Copyright Google LLC,
licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
tools/make_emoji.py builds the pack and the emoji table.

The emoji table follows the Unicode emoji data files, Copyright Unicode, Inc.,
https://www.unicode.org/terms_of_use.html

WebP pictures are decoded with libwebp, Copyright Google Inc., BSD license, third_party/libwebp/COPYING.
